Vision in one line: open, community-built software that lets a mid-level healthcare worker **interpret any imaging study at specialist level without escalation** — cheaper than a salaried radiologist and equally reliable. Training is crowdsourced: volunteers train models on their own hardware; the roadmap only defines *what* and *how*, never relying on a single machine.
